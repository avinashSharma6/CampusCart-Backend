Marketplace rules

Help keep CampusCart useful, respectful, and safer for students.

1. Be honest about your items

Use accurate titles, descriptions, prices, condition labels, and photos. Disclose defects and damage clearly.

2. List only items you're allowed to sell

Do not list stolen goods, counterfeit products, illegal items, dangerous goods, or anything prohibited by applicable law or university policy.

3. Meet thoughtfully

Arrange meetings in a public, well-lit place. Where possible, meet during the day, let someone you trust know your plans, and inspect the item before paying.

4. Protect personal information

Share only the information necessary to arrange a transaction. Never share passwords, verification codes, or banking credentials.

5. Report suspicious listings

If a listing appears misleading, prohibited, or suspicious, report it when the reporting feature is available. Do not complete a transaction if something feels wrong.

CampusCart is a platform for connecting buyers and sellers. Users should independently verify items and transaction details. These guidelines do not replace applicable law or university policy.
