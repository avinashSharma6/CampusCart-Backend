About us

Making campus life a little easier.

CampusCart is a student-focused marketplace designed to help university students buy and sell useful items within their campus community.

What can you find?

Textbooks, calculators, electronics, lab equipment, stationery, hostel essentials, sports equipment, bags, and other everyday student necessities.

Built around student needs

Students can publish listings, explore items by category, search for products, and contact sellers to arrange a transaction. The goal is to make it easier to find useful second-hand items while helping unwanted items find a new home.

Shop thoughtfully

Listings are provided by their sellers. Review item descriptions carefully, ask questions before meeting, and inspect an item before paying. Read our marketplace rules for additional safety guidance.
